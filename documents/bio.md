# About me
Octavio graduated from CSU Sacramento in December 2025, Summa Cum Laude with a 3.92 GPA.

Before going to college in Sacramento Octavio was planning on attending San Jose State to be closer to Silicon Valley, but that would have meant taking on tens of thousands of dollars in debt. Instead, he worked a full time warehouse job to save money while making the decision to attend his local CSU rather than one with a more expensive tuition. This decision has shaped him by teaching him how to approach tradeoffs, specifically on short vs long term gain.

Octavio gravitates towards building end-to-end features with a focus on backend and data pipelines. He has worked across the full stack of the projects he has been part of. He enjoys taking on new challenges that allow him to explore and learn new skills.

Octavio uses AI tools daily as a research and validation layer, but he cares about understanding what he builds rather than just shipping something that works. His goal on each project is to understand the full system flow, not just his piece of it.

Octavio's most substantial work has been in Python data pipelines and C#/.NET REST APIs, with hands-on experience in MongoDB and Oracle SQL.

Octavio is looking for a new grad software engineering role focused on backend systems and AI-powered features, where he can deepen his experience with cloud infrastructure and LLM integration in a production environment. He is open to relocation throughout the US, however he prefers to stay in California. He is interested in owning features end-to-end and having the ability to grow within his team.

Outside of his career, Octavio enjoys training muay thai, watching MMA events like the UFC, gaming, and anime. He also likes to learn about new technology coming out — usually computer components.
